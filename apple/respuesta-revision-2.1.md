Hello, thank you for the review. Here is the information requested.

1. Screen recording: attached. Recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS, starting from app launch and showing the main flow: new game → players and buy-ins → adding a player mid-game → rebuys → one player buying chips from another, with the running pot and movement log. At the end of the night "Contar fichas" asks how much each player has and "Ver el reparto" shows who pays whom. The app has no account registration or login, no user-generated content shared with other users, and no paid content or in-app purchases.

2. Purpose and audience: Bote is a calculator for friends who play poker at home with physical chips. At the end of the night someone always has to work out who owes whom. Bote records each player's buy-ins and rebuys, lets you type how much each player ends with (as money or as chips by colour), checks that the count matches the money put on the table, and computes the settlement with the minimum number of payments. It also suggests how to split the chips in the box so each stack adds up to the buy-in (with an option to give more or fewer chips of a given colour). Audience: adults who host casual home games.

3. How to use it: no login, credentials or sample files are needed. Open the app → "Nueva partida" → add two or more players with a buy-in → (optional) add a rebuy → "Contar fichas" and type how much money each player ends with (or switch to "Fichas" to count chips by colour) → "Ver el reparto" shows who pays whom → "Compartir" opens the system share sheet. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iOS 27 or later) there is an optional "Contar con una foto" button that takes a photo of the chips and pre-fills the counts, which the user can always correct by hand; on other devices the button does not appear and counts are typed in.

4. External services: none. All content is bundled inside the app and it works offline. It makes no network requests, has no analytics, ads, authentication, payment processor or third-party AI service. It only uses native iOS features: the share sheet, haptic feedback and, for the optional photo count, the camera and Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework, which runs entirely on the iPhone. Photos are not saved or sent anywhere. Data stays on the device.

5. Regional differences: none. The app works the same in every region.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material: not applicable. Bote is not a gambling app: it takes no bets, holds no money, processes no payments and offers no real or simulated casino games. It only does arithmetic on chip counts that users type in. It contains no third-party protected material.
